Figure X. Benchmark flow used for the matched comparison of microelectronic and skyrmion implementations. The same RTL benchmark is first synthesized and mapped to a CMOS standard-cell library or to the supported skyrmion gate set. The CMOS netlist is processed by OpenLane, whereas the skyrmion netlist is parsed and reconstructed with the gate/racetrack model. Both branches produce the same comparison dimensions: critical-path latency, energy/power, and implementation footprint. The CMOS footprint is obtained from the reported core/die area; the mapped skyrmion gate and track count is retained as the corresponding area proxy. The two result sets are compared for identical RTL functionality and an explicitly matched metric set.
